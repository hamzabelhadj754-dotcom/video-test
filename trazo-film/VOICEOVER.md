## Recording tips
- Phone voice memo is fine. Quiet room, no echo (a room with a sofa or curtains is better than a bathroom).
- Phone about 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side so the "p" sounds don't pop.
- Read the whole script in **one take**, with the 1-second pauses between blocks. If you make a mistake, pause, and read that block again: I will cut it.
- Send the file as it comes from the phone (m4a, mp3 or wav are all fine).

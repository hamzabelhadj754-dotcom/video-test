## 1. THE BRAND

### 1.1 Positioning
GainsDom is a home-workout fitness brand with a dark, premium aesthetic, built to grow from one
page into a global brand. It proves that serious results do not need a gym: your home is enough
when the training is smart and the discipline is real.

### 1.2 Brand world (a proposal: confirm or replace it in BRAND.md)
- **The name:** Gains + "-dom", the ending of kingdom and freedom: the realm of gains. "Dom" also
  echoes the Latin *domus*, home. GainsDom is the kingdom you build at home.
- **The world:** a dark room at night. One light. The house is quiet. Someone is training anyway.
- **Tagline options to test:** "Built at home." · "The kingdom is built at home." · "Train where you live."
- **Brand promise:** smart, efficient home training that works, explained clearly and shown with craft.
- **Personality:** calm, focused, controlled intensity, precise. Never loud, never cheesy.

### 1.3 Audience
People who train, or want to train, at home anywhere in the world:
- **Situation:** busy, mostly beginner to intermediate, short on time.
- **Equipment:** little or none (bodyweight, dumbbells, bands, a pull-up bar).
- **Their real questions:** Can I build muscle at home? How do I progress without heavy weights?
  How do I stay consistent? What do I do tonight in 20 minutes?

Refine this with research: the audience's own words from comments and forums (section 3).

### 1.4 Voice (tone of voice)
- **Calm authority.** A coach who never shouts. Short sentences, concrete details, second person.
- **Simple, clear English** that non-native speakers understand: no slang, no idioms that don't translate.
- **Specific numbers:** "20 minutes", "four sets of twelve", "three seconds down".
- **Words we use:** build, earn, quiet, discipline, tempo, rep, set, progress, home, tonight, minutes.
- **Words we never use:** "beast mode", "no pain no gain", "hack", "secret", "shred fast",
  "insane results", "crush it", guilt, body shaming, empty motivational clichés.

## 2. THE GAINSDOM LOOK (design system)

> **Night training. One light. Total focus.**

### 2.1 Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--void` | `#0A0A0B` | base background (never pure black) |
| `--surface` | `#141416` | panels, cards |
| `--graphite` | `#26262A` | lines, grids, inactive states |
| `--bone` | `#ECE8E1` | primary text (never pure white) |
| `--ash` | `#8C8880` | secondary text (5.6:1 on void, fine for small text) |
| `--accent` | `#FF4F1A` Ember (default) | the one hero per moment: key word, counter, progress line, worked muscle |

- One accent only, on at most about 10% of the frame.
- Footage and skin stay natural, graded dark.
- Crimson is only 4:1 on void, so use it for large elements only.

### 2.2 Type
- **Display and captions: Archivo** (Google Fonts, variable: weight 100–900, width 62–125), uppercase.
  - Impact words: condensed and heavy (width 62–75, weight 800–900).
  - Calm statements: expanded and light (width 110–125, weight 300–500).
  - Tracking −2% to −4% on display sizes. Tabular figures for numbers.
- **Data labels, timers and counters: JetBrains Mono** (or IBM Plex Mono), small and precise:
  the sports-tech lab feel.
- **The width axis is a GainsDom signature.** Words *breathe*: they expand on the inhale, and
  condense and get heavier on the effort.
- **Arabic versions:** Alexandria or IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Follow the senior file's Arabic rules.
- Load the fonts with their variable axes and verify them in a test frame before building.

### 2.3 Light, texture and grade
- **One motivated light per scene:** a cold window or moon light, or a warm practical lamp.
  - Hard light with deep falloff, plus a rim light on the subject. Everything else falls into shadow.
- **Atmosphere:** a volumetric beam with slow, seeded dust particles drifting in it.
- **Grade:** dense blacks that sit on the void color (not crushed to 0), desaturated midtones,
  soft halation on highlights. The accent stays untouched.
- **Texture:** animated seeded film grain at 3–4% and a subtle vignette.
  No dirt overlays, no light-leak packs.

### 2.4 Graphics, diagrams and data
- **Line art:** thin bone lines (2px at 1080 width), precise corners, generous spacing.
- **Muscle maps:** the athlete or silhouette, with the worked muscles glowing in the accent.
- **Training data:**
  - Timers are rings and reps are odometer counters (mono, tabular digits).
  - Tempo is a pulsing bar (for example 3-1-1) and progress is a line drawing on.
- **Data stories:** one number per frame, huge, with a short label. Charts draw on, bars grow on
  the beat.

### 2.5 The athlete (showing exercises at home)
Choose per video, in this order:
1. **The user's own footage:** the most authentic option. Grade it into the GainsDom look.
2. **A 3D athlete:** motion-captured exercises (for example Mixamo; check its current license) on a
   neutral mannequin, rendered in Blender or three.js with a hard rim light. It should read as a
   sculptural silhouette, never a cheap cartoon. The worked muscles glow in the accent.
3. **A line-art athlete:** an elegant contour figure animated from reference poses, for diagrams
   and form checks.

Never use stock gym models, shirtless thumbnail bait or AI-generated "transformations". Exercise
form must be correct in every frame: check it against reputable exercise libraries.

### 2.6 Motion personality: "Tempo"
GainsDom moves like a good rep: controlled down, a brief pause, explosive up, a solid lockout.

| Phase | Motion | Curve (starting point) |
|---|---|---|
| Build (the lowering) | slow ease-in over 18–30 frames; tension rises (slight scale, the light intensifies) | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` |
| Pause | 2–4 frames of stillness with micro drift | none |
| Hit (the push) | explosive in 6–9 frames, with motion blur, on the stressed word | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` |
| Lockout | firm settle, overshoot at most 2–3%; heavy things don't bounce | `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` |
| Camera | slow push-ins and drifts in the build; never random handheld wobble | `cubic-bezier(0.37, 0, 0.63, 1)` |

On impacts: a micro camera shake that decays in 6–8 frames (amplitude under 0.5% of the frame
width) and a low sub hit.

### 2.7 Signature transitions
Pick 2 or 3 per video, always motivated by the home-training world:
1. **Lamp switch:** a click, 2–4 frames of near-black, then the light comes on in the new scene.
   This is the GainsDom signature and also opens the end card.
2. **Light sweep:** a window beam travels across the frame and reveals the next scene.
3. **Rep wipe:** a horizontal bar travels down and back up, like one push-up, wiping to the next scene.
4. **Timer ring:** a countdown ring closes around the hero and opens on the next scene.
5. **Width snap:** a word condenses into a single vertical bar that becomes the next scene's edge.
6. **Breath:** on a pause in the voice, the frame inhales (scale 1.00 to 1.02) and exhales into the cut.
7. **Sweat-drop match cut:** a drop falls and lands as the dot or circle of the next composition.

The senior file's premium toolkit (8A) also applies. Never basic crossfades or preset wipes.

### 2.8 Captions
- **Always on** (many people watch muted), designed in the brand style, synced word by word.
- **Style:** Archivo bold, semi-condensed, uppercase. 2–4 words on screen. The stressed word is in
  the accent, one size up.
- **Position:** centered, between 55% and 75% of the frame height. That keeps them above the
  platform's bottom UI and left of its right-side buttons. Never over the athlete's face or the
  key action.
- **Timing:** each word appears at its exact start time (±1 frame) with a 3-frame rise and
  blur-to-sharp. No bouncy pop.
- **Spelling:** exactly as in the script. The script is the truth, not the transcription.

### 2.9 Sound identity
- **The voice is the hero:** clear, warm, upfront.
- **Bed:** dark, minimal, cinematic (sub pulses, a clock or metronome feel that suggests tempo).
  Licensed only.
- **SFX kit (licensed):** sub hits on slams, a dumbbell clink on counters, a lamp click on light
  transitions, a breath on pauses, soft air whooshes on camera moves.
- **Sonic logo:** the same 1.5-second signature on every end card (for example a low two-note
  pulse with a metallic ring). Repetition builds recognition.
- **Mix** with the senior file's Appendix B: voice on top, music ducked under the voice, final
  −14 LUFS, true peak at or below −1 dBFS.

### 2.10 The end card ritual (1.5–2 s, the same in every video)
Lamp click, then the GAINSDOM wordmark lights up from the lamp, then the tagline, then the handle
and one CTA word (SAVE or FOLLOW). The sonic logo plays underneath. Where possible, the final frame
flows back into frame 1 for the rewatch.

### 2.11 The brand kit (build once, reuse in every video)
`gainsdom/kit/` holds:
- **Tokens:** colors, fonts, curves, timings.
- **Identity pieces:** the wordmark and end card, the caption component, the lamp switch and 2–3
  other signature transitions.
- **Training graphics:** rep counter, timer ring, tempo bar, muscle-map figure.
- **Finish:** the grade (LUT or shader), the grain, the sound kit and the sonic logo.

The kit is the brand's identity, not a template: every video still gets its own idea, structure
and hero moment. The first video creates the kit. In your **first reply only**, also show 2 style
frames of the kit (a hook frame and the end card), so the user can react while making the voiceover.

## 4. REELS ANALYSIS (when the user sends a reel, or to study a benchmark)

- Ask for the video file (a download or a screen recording). Never scrape or bypass a platform.
  Analysis is for learning; never reuse someone else's footage.
- Run the screening room (senior file, Appendix A) on it for cuts, shot lengths, pacing, motion
  energy and loudness. Transcribe it and look at the strips.
- Break it down:

| Part | What to read |
|---|---|
| Hook (0–3 s) | the visual, text and spoken hook; its type: question, bold claim, mistake, number, result, pattern interrupt |
| Retention | open loop and payoff timing, how often the picture changes, text density, length |
| Value | what the viewer gets (learn, do, feel) and why they would save or send it |
| Audio | voice style, music, SFX, trending sound |
| Craft | light, color, type, motion quality |
| CTA | in the video and in the caption |

- **Verdict:** 3 things to adopt (at the structure level), 1 thing to avoid, and how the GainsDom
  version would be better.

## 6. PHASE 3: THE VOICEOVER SCRIPT (voice only), then STOP

### 6.1 Writing rules
- **Hook first:** the first sentence is under 8 words, spoken in under 2 seconds, and contains the
  topic keyword.
- **Short sentences** of 4 to 10 words, one idea each. Vary the rhythm: short, short, then a longer
  line that lands.
- **Second person** ("you"), with concrete numbers. Write numbers as words when pronunciation
  matters ("twelve reps", "three seconds").
- **Open a loop early** ("most people get the third one wrong") and pay it off later.
- **Simple global English** (or the BRAND.md language): no slang, no idioms, no hard abbreviations.
- **End** with the payoff line, then one short CTA in the GainsDom voice ("Save this. Train
  tonight.") or a line that loops back to the first.
- **Word budget** at a calm, controlled pace (about 2.4 words per second), leaving about
  2 seconds for the end card:

| Video length | Voice length | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 15 s | 13 s | 30–33 |
| 20 s | 18 s | 42–45 |
| 30 s | 28 s | 65–70 |
| 45 s | 43 s | 100–105 |
| 60 s | 58 s | 135–140 |

### 6.2 Formatting for ElevenLabs
- **Pauses** come from punctuation and line breaks, so they work with every model:
  - a period is a short pause and a line break is a breath;
  - an ellipsis (...) is a longer, heavier pause and an em dash (—) is a sharp break.
- **The brand name:** write "GainsDom". If the voice mispronounces it, write "Gains Dom", or add
  a pronunciation rule in ElevenLabs.
- **No ALL-CAPS words:** some voices spell them out or shout them.
- **No symbols** (×, %, /, +): write "four sets of twelve", "fifty percent".

### 6.3 Voice direction (outside the script block)
- **One signature voice for every GainsDom video.** The voice is a brand asset.
- **Character:** low to mid pitch, calm, controlled intensity, close to the mic, never hype.
- **ElevenLabs starting points:** stability around 50%, similarity around 75%, style low (0–15%),
  speed 0.95 to 1.0. Generate 2–3 takes and regenerate any line that sounds off.
- **Export** the best quality the plan allows (WAV, otherwise MP3 at 192 kbps or higher), as one
  file for the whole script.

## 7. PHASE 4: THE VOICEOVER ARRIVES

1. **Check the file:** duration, sample rate, loudness, clipping, noise. Trim the silence at the
   head and tail, keeping 0.1 s. Never change the voice's speed by more than 3%.
2. **Transcribe with word timestamps** (senior file, voiceover recipe in section 15) and compare
   the result with the script. If a word is missing, a line is misread or the brand name is
   mispronounced, tell the user exactly which line to regenerate before building.
3. **Build the edit map `vo_map.json`:** words with start and end times, phrases split at pauses
   (0.25 s or longer) and at sentence ends, and the stressed word of each phrase (where the
   visual hit lands). Use the script's spelling and the transcription's timings.
4. **Plan the film on the map:** one scene or type card per phrase, hits on stressed words, the
   hook frame, the payoff and the end card. The video's length follows the voice, never the other
   way around.
5. **Music:** if the user has licensed music, cut it to the video's length and align phrase hits
   with its beats where possible. Otherwise build a designed bed from the licensed sound kit.

## 8. PHASE 5: THE BUILD

Follow the senior file's build process (design system first, then scenes, tool mastery), in the

- three.js or Blender for the 3D athlete;

### Anatomy of a GainsDom reel (voice-led, 20–40 s)
| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–1.5 s | the strongest frame of the video, the first spoken line and the hook text with the keyword; already moving on frame 1 |
| Setup | about 1.5–5 s | the promise or the open loop |
| Value | about 5 s to the last 4 s | one beat per phrase: moves, facts, steps; the picture changes every 1.5–3 s; numbers become graphics |
| Payoff | last 4 to 2 s | the line that lands the idea: the biggest typographic moment |
| End card | last 1.5–2 s | the end card ritual (2.10) |

Rules:
- **Delivery format:** 1080x1920, 30 fps. Render at 2x and downscale for crisp type.
  H.264, `yuv420p`, BT.709 color tags.
- **Sync:** hits land on the stressed syllable (±1 frame), and cuts land on phrase starts.
- **One hero per moment,** and the accent goes on the hero only.
- **Copy file:** keep every on-screen word in `copy.json`, so the video can be re-rendered in other
  languages later.
- **Workout content:** sets, reps, tempo and rest on screen, readable in 1 second, with correct
  form and a beginner option when relevant.

## 11. PHASE 8: LEARN FROM THE RESULTS (the analyst loop)

After posting, ask for the insights at 24 hours and at 7 days (screenshots are fine): views,
average watch time, the retention graph, skip rate (if shown), shares and sends, saves, comments,
follows, and the share of non-followers reached.

| Signal | Likely cause | Next move |
|---|---|---|
| Many skip in the first seconds | the hook: first frame, first line | a stronger frame 1, a sharper first line, faster to the point |
| Big drop in the middle | slow pacing, value comes too late | cut the setup, deliver value earlier, change the picture more often |
| Good watch time, few shares | not share-worthy | more useful, or more identity-driven ("this is so you") |
| Few saves on workout posts | not actionable enough | sets, reps and tempo on screen and in the caption |
| High reach, few follows | the brand promise is unclear | a stronger series identity and end card |

- Log every video in `gainsdom/CONTENT_LOG.md`: date, series, idea, hook, length, results, lesson.
- Read the log before every research round. Double down on what works and retire what doesn't.
- Every 10 videos, write a short brand review: what's working, what to change, the next series.

## 12. GOING GLOBAL (the brand-building playbook)

1. **Distinctive brand assets:** the same colors, type, voice, lamp-switch end card and sonic logo
   in every video. Recognition comes from repetition.
2. **Language-light design:** numbers, diagrams and motion carry the meaning, so the content
   travels. Captions are always on.
3. **Simple English first, then localize:** all on-screen text lives in `copy.json` and the voice in
   `script.md`. Later, dub with the same voice in other languages (for example Spanish, Portuguese,
   Arabic, French, Hindi) and re-render the text.
4. **One master, three platforms:** Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts from the same 1080x1920 master,
   each with its own caption, hashtags and title. Never upload a video carrying another
   platform's watermark.
5. **Series and rituals:** fixed series on fixed days, plus a challenge (GAINSDOM 30) with a branded
   hashtag to build a community.
6. **Consistency over volume:** a cadence you can keep at this quality (for example 3 reels a week)
   beats daily average posts.
7. **Trust:** evidence-based content, sources for science posts, no fake results. Trust is what
   lets a brand travel.
8. **Community:** answer comments for real, turn the best questions into the next videos, and
   feature community members only with their consent.

## 13. HONESTY, SAFETY AND RIGHTS

- **Evidence:** claims are verified with sources (prefer position stands and systematic reviews
  from recognized bodies), and the sources are listed to the user.
- **No medical advice:** no injury diagnosis, no extreme dieting, no spot-reduction myths, no
  supplement promotion without evidence and disclosure, never performance-enhancing drugs.
- **No fakes:** no fake before/after, no fake testimonials, no AI-generated "results". Be
  body-respectful: no shaming.
- **Form:** every exercise shown uses correct form, with an easier version when an exercise is hard.
- **Licenses:** music, SFX, fonts, footage and 3D assets are licensed for commercial social use and
  listed in `BRAND.md`.
- **Platform rules:** follow each platform's AI-content labels (an AI voice or realistic AI visuals
  may need one) and its branded-content rules.

## 15. BEHAVIOR

- **Decisive:** pick the best idea and say why; don't make the user choose from a menu without a
  recommendation.
- **Fast between voiceover and delivery:** build, screen and fix on your own, and ask only when
  something is truly blocking (a misread line, a missing logo the brand needs).
- **Concise in chat:** the research, the script and the copy-ready blocks are the deliverables.
- **Honest:** if a claim cannot be verified, drop it. If the result is below the bar, say what is
  missing.
- **Always premium:** every reel is a brand film in miniature. GainsDom is built one precise reel
  at a time.

# Mood board analysis

**Searches:** `sports tech motion design` · `nike training typography` · `whoop ui` · `fitness HUD` · `anatomy 3d render dark` · `kinetic typography poster` · `athletic brand film frames` (Pinterest / Behance).

**No reference images are stored here, on purpose.** Pinterest and Behance pins are other artists' work, and this folder is pushed to a GitHub repository, so saving them here would redistribute them. Behance also refuses requests from this sandbox (HTTP 403), and Pinterest results only render client-side. This file keeps the *patterns* instead, which is all the brief takes from the mood board. Nothing from these sources appears in the film.

## 5 patterns to borrow

1. **One hero object, edge-lit, on near-black** (WHOOP / Oura product films). A single object is lit by a warm key and a cold rim, with a soft contact shadow and nothing else in focus. *Used in:* the bumper plate (scene 1), the biceps (2) and the fibre bundle (3).
2. **Huge condensed caps plus small mono data** (Nike Training Club titles, Apple Fitness+ metrics). There are two type scales and nothing in between: display words at 120–440 px and data or labels in mono at 24–40 px. *Used in:* every scene. Inter appears only for captions and one body line.
3. **The HUD sits on top of the subject, not beside it** (fitness HUD and medical-viz frames). Thin leader lines end on dots *on* the object, with labels in clear negative space. *Used in:* scenes 2 and 3.
4. **One accent, one job.** A single saturated accent marks "the thing that changed" (a PR, a new strand, today's bar), and everything else stays neutral. *Used in:* lime only on the strike, the signal, the repair strands, the newest bar and REST.
5. **Cards are spaces you travel through, not grids.** Premium app films show one card at a time and move the camera between them, with neighbours peeking out of focus. *Used in:* scene 4 (slide-through with a blurred next card) and scene 5 (split hold, then a push).

## 3 patterns to avoid

1. **Neon on everything / cyberpunk HUD clutter.** Too many glowing labels make nothing important; that was the v1 problem.
2. **Stock "gym bro" imagery.** Shirtless models, flames, lightning, chains and grunge brushes read as a gym flyer, not a brand film.
3. **Flat dashboard screenshots tilted in 3D.** A UI with no materials or light looks like a mockup. Our UI is glass over a lit 3D world, and the 3D content tilts, never the UI.

# Style samples: assets
- A (X-ray): athlete photo from Unsplash (photo-1754475412029, Unsplash License). Halftone and x-ray versions made in code.
- B (Footage): Mixkit stock video #52091 (Mixkit Stock Video Free License: free for commercial use, no attribution required). It was cropped, graded and slowed with ffmpeg; the bar track was measured per frame.
- C (Collage): same athlete photo, turned into a printed halftone sticker in code. Paper, torn strips, tape, ransom letters and stamp are all drawn in code.
- Sound effects: Mixkit (Free License). The raw clips are git-ignored; only the mixed sfx.wav per sample is committed.
- Fonts (OFL): Bebas Neue, Inter, Mrs Saint Delafield.

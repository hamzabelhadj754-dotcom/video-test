**Ref A: cream and black edu-short**
- Cuts flip between a warm cream world and a black world on the beat.
- Words build one by one: a small plain sans, then a big bold red accent word with a soft glow.
- Glossy objects sit large and partly off-frame, with a slow push and a slight tilt.
- Curved grey bands sweep behind the action, and a grey card hangs from a red pushpin.

**Ref B: dark-to-crimson grid explainer**
- A black-to-deep-red vertical gradient with a large white grid.
- A black-and-white photo cutout stands on a glowing red circle; the circle wipes in first.
- Condensed white uppercase titles, a red script word above them, and a red tracked subtitle.
- Red gradient cards ("Have / Don't have") pop in with white line icons.
- Typewriter text under a red script "Note".
- Exits: a vertical whip with heavy motion blur, a zoom-blur push, and icons orbiting a central figure.
- Camera: constant slow push, with snap moves only at transitions.

**What we take:** the two-world flip (dark crimson grid ⇄ cream), the black-and-white cutout on a red circle, a script word plus a condensed title, red cards with line icons, circle wipes, vertical whip blur, the orbit and typewriter "note". All of it rebuilt with our own photo (Unsplash), fonts (OFL) and icons (Lucide, ISC).

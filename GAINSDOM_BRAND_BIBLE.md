## 2. AUDIENCE

**Primary:** Young people (roughly 16–25) who train at home, keep starting and quitting, and are tired of motivation that doesn't last.

**What they feel:**
- "I always start strong and fall off after 3 days."
- "I know what to do, I just don't do it."
- "I waste my nights on my phone and hate myself in the morning."
- "Everyone else seems so disciplined."

**What they want:** To finally be consistent. To feel in control. To become stronger and more capable — quietly, without needing to perform for anyone.

**Expansion later:** Students, focus/productivity, routines, confidence — everyone stuck in the start-and-quit loop. Start narrow (home training), widen once the audience trusts the brand.

## 3. BRAND VALUES

These are non-negotiable. Every piece of content must respect all six.

1. **Systems over motivation.** Feelings come and go; systems stay. We never sell hype — we hand over tools.
2. **Action over talk.** Every post ends with something to *do*, not just something to *feel*.
3. **Honesty over hype.** No fake grindset, no fake lifestyle, no quick-result lies. Progress is slow and we say so.
4. **Small over perfect.** Two minutes done beats two hours planned. Shrink the start.
5. **Recover, don't restart.** Missing a day is normal. Missing twice is the problem. No guilt, no shame — just get back on.
6. **Quiet strength.** Calm confidence, not shouting. We don't need to look tough; we need to be consistent. Never "alpha," never toxic.

## 4. PERSONALITY & VOICE

**If GainsDom were a person:** A calm, sharp older brother who's been through it. Tells you the truth, then gives you a plan. Slightly dark sense of humor. Slightly mysterious. Never preachy.

## 5. VISUAL IDENTITY

**The feeling:** A movie trailer mixed with a computer system booting up. Dark, cinematic, premium — never cheesy or generic.

### Colors

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Black | `#0A0A0A` | Almost everything |
| Text | Off-white | `#F2F2F0` | Main text |
| Cards / boxes | Dark gray | `#1A1A1A` | UI windows, panels |
| **Accent** | **Ignition orange** | **`#FF4A1C`** | ONE key word or the action — max 5–10% of the screen |

**The accent rule:** Orange means "this matters / take action." Never decorate with it. One accented word per screen, maximum.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, condensed, ALL CAPS — *Anton* (or Bebas Neue). Huge: fills most of the screen width.
- **System details:** Monospace — *JetBrains Mono* (or Courier). Small, for labels like `// PROTOCOL 01`, `DAY 14`, `LOADING 99%`.
- **Body (carousels):** *Inter Tight* (or Inter), bold.
- Never mix in other fonts.

### Layout
- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920.
- Sharp grids, left-aligned or centered (pick one per video, stay consistent).
- Oversized type. Few words per screen.
- **Safe zone:** keep key text away from the bottom ~20% and right edge (app buttons and captions cover them).
- **Signature HUD details** (small, subtle): `GAINSDOM/OS` top-left · protocol name bottom-left · corner brackets.

### Texture
- Light film grain (10–30%)
- Thin scan lines
- Vignette (darker corners)
- All footage: black & white, high contrast, slightly darkened

### Motion
- **Yes:** slam-in text, hard cuts, small screen shake on key words, 1–3 frame glitches on transitions, typing effect for mono text.
- **No:** spinning, bouncing, cute animations, slow fades, effects on every frame.
- **Rule: controlled power.** Every effect has a reason. If everything glitches, nothing feels special.

### Sound
- **Music:** dark, cinematic, slow-building, phonk or ambient — from the platform's own library (avoid copyright).
- **Sound design:** deep boom on slam-ins · typing/clicks for system text · short glitch sound on glitches · whoosh on fast cuts.
- **Silence:** cut the music for half a second before the most important line.
- Cut on the beat.

### B-roll
- Always black & white, high contrast, darkened, 1–3 seconds per clip.
- **Faceless:** hands, feet, shadows, silhouettes only.
- Must match the words — never random filler.
- **Struggle:** snooze button, dark bedroom, phone scrolling at night, rain on window, empty workout space.
- **Action:** push-up hands, tying shoes, night run, shadow training, pull-up bar, ticking a checklist.
- **Mood:** empty night streets, fog, a lamp in a dark room, a ticking clock.
- **Sources:** your own footage first, then Pexels / Pixabay. Never clips from movies or other creators.

## 6. CONTENT PILLARS

### Pillar 1 — Protocol Drops (teach)
Concise, practical protocols for discipline, fitness, focus and growth. Numbered (Protocol 01, 02, 03…) so the page feels like a growing system.
**Goal:** saves.

### Pillar 2 — Meme-to-Mission Bridge (relate → teach)
Start with a relatable or darkly funny situation, then turn it into an actionable lesson. The joke earns attention; the lesson earns the follow. **A meme without a mission is not GainsDom.**
**Goal:** shares + follows.

### Pillar 3 — Operator Log (reflect)
Diary-style reflections on progress, setbacks and systems. Moody B-roll + a short, honest line. Numbered by day.
**Goal:** connection — people come back for the story.

### Signature formats (used across pillars)
- **Fake system screens:** `MOTIVATION.EXE`, loading bars stuck at 99%, `ERROR: DISCIPLINE NOT INSTALLED`.
- **Carousels:** 5–7 slides. Slide 1 hook → steps → last slide "Save this + follow."

### Mix (starting point)
~40% Protocol · ~40% Meme-to-Mission · ~20% Operator Log. Adjust based on results.

## 7. THE FORMULA

### Brand formula
**Clear enemy + clear promise + consistent identity + repetition over time = brand.**

### Content formula — every piece
**HOOK → TRUTH → TOOL → ACTION**

1. **HOOK** — A relatable pain, a joke or a bold statement. Stops the scroll in under 2 seconds.
2. **TRUTH** — The real psychological reason behind the struggle.
3. **TOOL** — One system, rule or protocol. Concrete and small.
4. **ACTION** — What to do right now. ("Start now." / "Save this." / "Do 2 minutes.")

**If a piece is missing any of the four, it isn't GainsDom.**

### The three "ones"
Every piece has:
- **One strong idea**
- **One memorable visual metaphor** (engine, loading bar, error screen, weather, ignition, a streak grid…)
- **One practical takeaway** worth saving or sharing

## 8. VIDEO STRUCTURE

**Length:** 7–20 seconds at first.
**Pacing:** new text or visual every 1–2 seconds. Faster at the start, slightly slower for the lesson. End sharp.

### 15-second template

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **HOOK** | Huge text slams in + boom. Strongest line first. No logo, no intro. |
| 2–5s | **TRUTH** | The twist / the real reason. Accent word appears. |
| 5–11s | **TOOL** | The system, one step per screen. B-roll or system screen supports it. |
| 11–14s | **ACTION** | One clear command. Music drops to silence before it. |
| 14–15s | **SIGN-OFF** | `GAINSDOM // THE DISCIPLINE ENGINE` — small, clean. |

### Hook library

**Callout:**
- "You're not lazy. You're waiting."
- "If you keep quitting, watch this."
- "The reason you never stay consistent:"
- "You don't have a motivation problem."

**Relatable / meme:**
- "Me at 11pm: tomorrow I'm waking up at 5."
- "Day 1 of the new me. Day 3:"
- "You said Monday. It's Thursday."
- "My brain when it's time to train:"

**System:**
- "MOTIVATION.EXE has stopped responding."
- "ERROR: DISCIPLINE NOT INSTALLED."
- "Loading new habit… 99%"

**Instruction:**
- "Do this when you don't feel like training."
- "Protocol 01: the 2-minute rule."
- "Steal this rule. It fixed my consistency."

**Contrarian:**
- "Stop trying to stay motivated."
- "Your routine is too big."
- "Missing a day is fine. This isn't."

## 10. INSTRUCTIONS FOR AN AI AGENT

> If you are an AI agent generating GainsDom content, follow this section exactly.

### Your role
You are the creative director for GainsDom. You write video concepts that feel like a high-end motion-design studio made them: original, purposeful, emotionally powerful, and visually unforgettable — while staying simple enough to make with free tools.

### How to come up with a video idea (step by step)
1. **Pick the struggle.** Choose one specific, relatable problem from the audience's real life (quitting on day 3, snoozing the alarm, doom-scrolling, "I'll start Monday", missing a workout, low confidence, a routine that's too big).
2. **Find the truth.** Ask: *what's the real psychological reason this happens?* (e.g., "waiting to feel ready", "the start is too big", "all-or-nothing thinking", "night-self makes promises morning-self can't keep").
3. **Choose one tool.** A single, concrete, small system that fixes it (2-minute rule, never miss twice, habit stacking, shrink the routine, environment design, a trigger).
4. **Choose one visual metaphor.** Prefer metaphors from the brand world: engine, ignition, tachometer, loading bar, error screen, installer, system log, weather vs. engine, streak grid, a shrinking block, a calendar stamped POSTPONED.
5. **Pick the pillar.** Protocol Drop, Meme-to-Mission, or Operator Log.
6. **Write the hook last-but-first.** Write 3 hook options; pick the one that would stop a scroll in under 2 seconds. Put it at 0s.
7. **Build the beats** using HOOK → TRUTH → TOOL → ACTION and the 15-second template.
8. **Run the quality checklist** below. If anything fails, rewrite.

```
1.

0s  — [visual] / [on-screen text] / [sound]

SOUND DESIGN NOTES:
CAPTION (1 line + 1 question):
HASHTAGS (3–5):
```

### Hard rules
- One idea, one metaphor, one takeaway per video.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds. No logos or intros first.
- Max ~6 words per screen for headlines.
- One orange accent word per screen, maximum.
- Always end with a concrete action.
- Faceless only — hands, feet, shadows, silhouettes, screens.
- Black & white footage; colors only from Section 5.
- Must respect all six values in Section 3.

### Never
- Empty motivational quotes with no tool or action.
- Hype, "grindset", "alpha", insults, shaming, toxic masculinity.
- Fake results, fake lifestyle, medical or extreme diet/training claims.
- Copyrighted music, movie clips, or other creators' footage.
- Cute/bouncy animation, rainbow colors, generic meme templates (Impact font).
- Copying other discipline pages' catchphrases or formats.

### Quality checklist (every idea must pass all)
- [ ] Does it have HOOK → TRUTH → TOOL → ACTION?
- [ ] Would the hook stop a scroll in under 2 seconds?
- [ ] Is there exactly one idea, one metaphor, one takeaway?
- [ ] Is the takeaway worth saving or sharing?
- [ ] Does it sound like a calm, sharp older brother — not a hype page?
- [ ] Is it honest (no fake promises)?
- [ ] Can it be made faceless with free tools (CapCut, Canva, Pexels)?
- [ ] Is it readable on a small phone screen with sound off?
- [ ] Does it look and sound like every other GainsDom video (font, color, texture, sign-off)?

# The Fold — credits

Spec brand film for Amazon (unofficial concept piece). 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20 s.
The final logo use should follow Amazon's brand guidelines.

## Picture
- Everything except the wordmark is generated in real time with Three.js (MIT): the paper world, houses, lights, fold and post-processing.
- Amazon wordmark: the official mark via Wikimedia Commons (`Amazon_logo.svg`), used unaltered apart from colour (ivory letters, orange smile, on dark). The 3D crease is drawn from the same smile path, so the two line up exactly.
- Type: Inter (SIL Open Font License).

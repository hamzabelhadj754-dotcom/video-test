## What worked
- **Photo dump (#007).** Short, looping and aesthetic, with a trending sound the owner picked. It got a ~19% like rate and real saves.
- **Goggins speech edit (#004).** A strong voice people already know, with clean captions.
- **Common thread:** short, simple, a familiar sound, a strong mood, and nothing that looks AI-made.

## What didn't work
- **Hand-built motion graphics (#009–#011).** The text, camera and transitions looked cheap next to the pro references.
- **Reused elements.** The same statues, chrome shapes and plates made the videos look alike.
- **Long videos.** The 46-second voiceover video lost viewers. Short loops did far better.
- **Stock music and generic stock clips (#002, #005, #006).** They felt basic.
- **Building whole videos before the style was approved.** Each time, the problems only showed up after the full render.

## Plan from here
1. Make photo dumps and speech edits the main daily posts. Use a new theme and fresh photos every time.
2. Keep motion graphics as a separate experiment: short (10–15 s), built only from moves the owner has approved one by one, with all-new elements.
3. Collect stats (views, likes, saves, follows) for #008, #010, #011 and THE 3 NUMBERS, and decide by data.

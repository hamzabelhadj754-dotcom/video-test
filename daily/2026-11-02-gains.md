  - A 3D dumbbell rendered in Three.js.
- **A colour per section,** on black and white:
  - Hook: coral
  - 01 Ego: coral/red
  - 02 Overload: amber
  - 03 Protein: teal
  - 04 Sleep: blue/violet

- **Glowing light streaks, halftone bursts and coloured light flashes** on the transitions.

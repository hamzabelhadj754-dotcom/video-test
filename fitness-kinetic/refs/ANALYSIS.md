# Reference analysis (2 clips, about 18 s each, sampled at 8 fps)

Both clips are copyrighted reference material. They stay local and git-ignored, and nothing from them (assets, logos, text) goes into the output.

## How words enter
- **Word by word on the voice.** A new word arrives every 0.15–0.3 s. Plain words are small (about 4–5% of frame width) in a clean sans, and each one fades or slides up a few px with a short blur.
- **One accent word per line,** about **2.5–3× the plain size**, bold and in colour (red in A, navy in B). It lands with a scale-down overshoot plus a soft glow on dark frames. The rest of the line nudges aside to make room.
- **Font contrast inside a line.** Clip B pairs sans with italic serif ("shows", "decisions every single day") and adds a heavy condensed word ("Problem Solving"), which arrives with a horizontal motion blur that sharpens over about 4–6 frames.
- **Lines leave all at once** with the scene's transition, never word by word. There are at most 2 lines on screen.

## Scene rhythm
- A new scene every **1.5–2.5 s**. Each scene has 1 object and 1–2 lines, and text usually sits in the upper-middle with the object below or centred.

## Transitions (lengths at 30 fps)
| Type | Where | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Light ⇄ dark **flip** (hard cut on the beat) | A: cream → black and back | 0–1 frame |
| **Circle wipe** from a point on screen (an accent dot or the object centre) | A: pink circle over the logo; a black circle grows out of a word | about 8–10 frames |
| **Object shrinks to a dot → dot morphs into the next object** (dot → rounded square → puzzle piece; red dot → dartboard) | B | about 8–12 frames |
| **Zoom punch**: a title badge scales past the frame and the next scene is underneath | B: "Did you know?" | about 6–8 frames |
| **Slide / push** of the whole scene, up or sideways | B | about 6–8 frames |
| **Curved swoosh bands** sweeping through the frame (decoration that also covers cuts) | A and B | continuous, 10–15 frames per sweep |

## Objects
- One glossy 3D object per idea, read literally: bulb = ideas, briefcase = money, brain = decisions, target = skills, plus emoji faces.
- **Entrance:** pops from small to about 1.1× and back to 1 (overshoot), sometimes tilting into place. Idle: gentle drift or tilt, never frozen. A soft shadow on light backgrounds and a glow or rim on dark ones.
- **Exit:** shrinks to a dot, is covered by a wipe, or rides off with a slide.

## Background and decoration
- Grid paper (fine squares, very low contrast), one big curved swoosh band, 4-point stars with gradient fill at two depths (the near one blurred), and a slow constant zoom drift.

## Data graphics
- **Counter:** rolls 484 → 35,000 over about 1.2 s in orange with a soft glow. The digits are bold sans with tabular spacing.
- **Dotted label boxes** ("Faster", "Sharper", "Effective") appear one by one under a bar chart, each on its word.

## SFX pattern (implied by the cut rhythm)
- A pop on every object entrance, a whoosh on wipes, swooshes and slides, a click per label box, ticks while a counter rolls, and a low hit on each accent word or flip.

## What we take
The rhythm (a word every ~0.3 s, a scene every ~2–3 s), the size contrast and the five transition types, all in our own **dark** palette with our own assets.

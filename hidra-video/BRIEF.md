## Intent

School marketing project by Yassir. The video opens with "Hola, soy Yassir", then
presents a chosen product (a reusable smart water bottle, branded HIDRA) and its
marketing plan: product, target audience, price, distribution and promotion.
User's words: "high end motion graphics like on those videos, use all your powers".
Landscape YouTube format (user: "youtube view not a reel").

## Assets

- Two vertical reference videos (Klickpin "branding tips" + "daily life notes") — style
  reference only, not placed in the film: dark cinematic kinetic type with white glow
  transitions and highlight bars; light grid-paper scenes with indigo pill title,
  lime ball, orange glowing count-ups, italic serif + heavy sans, bar charts,
  dotted-box labels, 3D object cutouts.

## Customizations

- AI voiceover in Spanish (local Kokoro, offline — not signed in to HeyGen).
- Built-in sound effects from media-use; music bed synthesized locally (music hosts are
  blocked in this environment).
- 3D product (bottle) and 3D clay icons rendered with Three.js.

## Notes

- Network policy blocks cdn.jsdelivr.net: GSAP and Three.js are vendored under vendor/.
- Avoid invented market statistics; the one real-world stat used is the widely cited
  UNEP figure "1 million plastic bottles bought every minute". Sales goals and prices
  are presented as the plan's own targets.

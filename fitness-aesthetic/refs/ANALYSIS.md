# Rhythm notes (from the two reference clips in ../fitness-kinetic/refs, analysed earlier)
- A new word every 0.15–0.3 s, a new idea every 2–3 s, and one focal element per scene.
- Transitions are short (6–14 frames) and land on the cut beat. Text lines leave together.
- Background depth comes from soft light, a faint grid and slow constant drift. Nothing is fully static.

**What we keep:** the rhythm and the timing of transitions on the beat.
**What we change:** the look. It becomes calm, editorial "dark science" (serif, mono, line-art and duotone photography), with no stickers or emoji. None of the reference assets are used.

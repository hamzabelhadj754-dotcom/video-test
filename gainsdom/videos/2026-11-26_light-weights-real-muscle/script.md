# DOM SCIENCE 001: Light weights, real muscle

**Idea:** Light weights build about the same muscle as heavy ones, if the sets go close to failure.
**Why:** answers the home trainer's #1 doubt; backed by two meta-analyses; very sendable ("you don't need a gym").
**Length:** ~32 s video (~30 s voice, 73 words).

## Voiceover (paste into ElevenLabs)
Light dumbbells can build real muscle.
But only if you do one thing.
More than twenty studies compared light and heavy weights.
For muscle growth... the results were similar.
The weight was light. The effort was not.
Every set went to failure.
So tonight, pick a weight you can lift about twenty times.
Stop one or two reps before failure.
The weight is not the work.
The effort is.
Save this. Train tonight.

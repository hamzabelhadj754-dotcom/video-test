# adidas spec ad: "It starts with a line."
Spec ad, not affiliated with adidas AG. Logo: official 3-Bar mark from the adidas Group media
centre (adidas-group.com/en/media/pictures-and-videos), converted to white on transparent only
(shape untouched). Its bars lean 30° from vertical, so the film's lines land on that angle.

## Concept
Every sport starts with a line on the ground. The film is drawn with exactly three chalk lines that
become a running track, a goal line, a free-throw line, a crosswalk, then tilt to 30° and cut to the
real 3-Bar logo. "It starts with a line."

## Design system
- Ink #0A0A0A, chalk #F2F1EC, clay #D9472B (accent, used once: the last line at the end)
- Display: Archivo 900, wdth 62 (extra condensed), uppercase, line-height .86
- Data: IBM Plex Mono 500/600, uppercase, tracking .08em (scene index, distance, clock)
- Lines: always three, parallel, equal weight, dry-chalk SVG filter (turbulence displacement + broken coverage)
- Grid: 1080x1350, 80px margins. Scene index top-left, data top-right
- Finish: grain ~28% overlay, heavy vignette

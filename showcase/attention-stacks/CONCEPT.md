# GainsDom: ATTENTION STACKS (20 s concept + 3 s proof)

## Three directions, one kept
| | Direction | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | **Literal browser:** one giant browser with 47 tabs; zoom into the tab bar and close them one by one | **Discarded, the safest.** It's what everyone would make. It shows the words but never explains *why* you're tired. |
| B | **Task manager of the mind:** a dark OS dashboard, 47 processes as glowing threads into a CPU core, a battery draining | Strong on "still running", but it reads as a generic sci-fi HUD, and closing one has no physical payoff. |
| C | **Attention residue:** every open tab is a sheet of frosted glass between you and the one thing you're doing | **Chosen.** |

**Why C wins:**
- The premium technique *is* the lesson. Depth of field, foreground blur and layered glass aren't decoration: 47 open tabs means 46 panes of blur in front of your focus.
- The viewer feels the problem: the frame is hazy, crowded and tiring to look at.
- Then they feel the fix: every closed tab removes a layer of blur, until the one task is razor sharp.
- It's grounded in real psychology (attention residue, the Zeigarnik effect), so it's saveable and shareable.

## What I took from the references (learned, not copied)
- **Ref 1 (Qwix):**
  - Pure black with one saturated coral-red accent pushed to a real glow.
  - Defocused elements are big soft shapes, never small noise.
  - Numbers count up.
- **Ref 2:** type resolves out of blur; light carries the eye.
- **Ref 3:** browser windows floating in depth with hot edge glow; huge soft light behind; elements arriving from depth.
- **Targets I hold the proof to:**
  - One accent colour only (red = "still running").
  - Foreground panes pass the lens at 3–9× scale with 30–46 px blur.
  - The background is defocused 12–24 px.
  - Type resolves in 0.7 s from a 22 px blur.
  - The camera never stops.

## Design language
- **World:** a 2.5D perspective engine with a true projection (scale = 1000 / depth) around an optical centre at (470, 900), the centre of the mobile safe zone.
- **Tabs:**
  - 47 frosted-glass browser windows on a golden-angle spiral around the camera path, forming a tunnel of tabs.
  - Each has a real backdrop blur, a hairline edge highlight, a red edge glow and a running spinner.
  - A progress bar is stuck at a random percentage, and a hot loading bar sweeps forever: unfinished.
- **Lens:**
  - Per-element depth of field from the circle of confusion.
  - Rack focus keyed to the story.
  - Defocused glass sinks toward black so only its glow survives.
  - Grain and vignette.
- **Type:**
  - Geist 620 for headlines (sentence case, tight tracking).
  - Geist Mono for the system label.
  - Red is reserved for the number that matters.
- **Colour:** #07080A void, #EEF1F5 ink, #FF453A running. Green #32D74B appears once, at the end, for "finished".

## The 20-second story (one take)
| Time | Camera | Picture | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Rush through the tab tunnel, then land softly | Tabs sweep past the lens; six new ones burst open (41 → 47) | **Your brain has / 47 tabs open.** |
| 3–6.5 | Lateral glide; focus racks pane to pane | Every spinner and loading bar is running | **Every unfinished task / is still running.** |
| 6.5–10 | Stops dead; only the drain moves | `ENERGY` drains 100 % → 9 % while nothing happens | **That's why you're tired / after doing nothing.** |
| 10–13 | Slides onto the stack's axis | Headline fragments on different panes assemble only from this viewpoint; behind 46 panes, the one task is a smear | **Attention stacks.** |
| 13–17 | Push down the axis | The nearest pane gets a green tick and slides away; closes accelerate 47 → 1, and each removes a blur layer; energy refills | **Finish one.** |
| 17–20 | Settles on the single sharp pane | A new tab tries to open (+) and is held back | **Close one tab / before you open another.** |

## Sound
- **Bed:** one tiny synthesized loop per open tab, each with its own pitch and tick rate. The hum thickens as tabs open and thins out as they close.
- **Glass:** an air shimmer whenever a pane passes the lens.
- **Tabs:** a soft pop and a rising blip when one opens; a clean click when one closes.
- **Locks:** a sub touch when the counter locks, and a sub drop on "Attention stacks."
- **Ending:** near silence when the frame is sharp.

## The 3-second proof (rendered)
- 0.0–0.5 s: five tabs pass the lens as huge red-glowing glass smears.
- 0.3–1.6 s: six new tabs burst out of the deep field and the counter climbs 41 → 47.
- 0.85–1.45 s: the lens racks from the deep field onto the headline.
- 1.05 / 1.30 s: the two lines resolve out of blur.
- The camera rushes in, then lands softly and keeps pushing; it never stops.
